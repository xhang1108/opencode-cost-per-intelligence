Discount method — OpenCode does not always label a discount; it often just lowers the price. We use AA's list price per 1M tokens as the reference — the most widely accepted public price today — and compute discount = 1 − OpenCode actual price / AA list price. Where AA list pricing is unavailable, the struck-through Console list price is used as the reference. This applies to Go models only and leaves the Original (list) price untouched. We keep this method unless a dispute requires a change.
